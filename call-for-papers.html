Call for Presentations

Present your recently accepted work from major conferences at ICVGIP, 3DV2XR 2026.

Scope

The workshop aims to bring the researchers to showcase their recent and high-quality research in 3D Vision and Extended Reality (XR). We invite authors of relevant papers that have already been accepted at or published in major conferences and venues for presenting at the 3DV2XR workshop. Interested authors can submit the details of their paper through the Google Form below. Based on the relevance of the submissions to the workshop themes, a selected set of papers will be invited for presentation during the workshop.

Topics of Interest

  • 3D reconstruction, multi-view stereo, SLAM, localization, and spatial mapping
  • Interactive and multimodal scene and asset generation: text-to-3D, image-to-3D, vision-language models for XR
  • Scene understanding (semantic, scene layout, spatial relationships) for user interaction and navigation
  • Human-computer interaction, embodied interaction, digital twins, smart cities, healthcare, education, training, and industrial XR
  • Interactive scene editing in immersive environments: User-controllable, constraint-aware editing
  • Real-time/low-latency neural rendering for immersive displays
  • Physics-aware interaction with scenes/objects

Important Dates
