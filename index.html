About

Bridging 3D Vision and Intelligent Extended Reality

Recent advances in 3D vision, generative AI, and spatial computing are transforming how digital systems perceive, reconstruct, understand, and interact with the physical world.

At the same time, Extended Reality is evolving beyond visualization toward intelligent, interactive and spatially aware applications.

Despite rapid progress in 3D vision and XR, research across these areas remains largely fragmented. Advances in reconstruction, generation, scene understanding, rendering, and interaction are often developed and evaluated independently, even though practical XR systems must integrate them into a unified perception, reasoning and interaction loop.

This workshop brings together researchers from computer vision, graphics, machine learning, robotics and XR to discuss technologies enabling the next generation of intelligent spatial computing systems. It will highlight open challenges, emerging research directions, and opportunities for translating advances in 3D vision into deployable real-world spatial computing applications.
