Keynote Speakers

Invited researchers working across 3D computer vision, generative AI, graphics and Extended Reality.

Prof. Avinash Sharma

Associate Professor · Department of Computer Science & Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Bombay

Prof. Avinash Sharma heads 3DVisLab at IIT Bombay. His research spans 3D computer vision, AR/VR, and computer graphics, including human and garment modeling, face and hand modeling, and point cloud analysis for autonomous navigation.

Kiran Nanjunda Iyer

Senior Principal Architect & Group Head of XR Perception
Samsung Research Institute Bangalore

Mr. Kiran Nanjunda Iyer leads the XR Perception R&D group, driving the development of core perception technologies for next-generation XR headsets and glasses. He has played a key leadership role in the development and commercialization of Samsung's first XR product.

Prior to his work in XR, he made significant contributions to Samsung Galaxy products. His research interests span 3D vision, spatial perception, multi-sensor systems and AI.

Dr. Rajendra Nagar

Assistant Professor · Department of Electrical Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur

Dr. Rajendra Nagar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Jodhpur. His research interests include 3D shape matching, point cloud correspondence estimation, and neural representations of 3D geometry.
